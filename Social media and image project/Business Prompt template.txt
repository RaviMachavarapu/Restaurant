✍️ Create Paragraph-Style Posts Based on These 6 Types:

Each type should feel natural and be written in paragraph format with a soft call-to-action at the end. Use simple, clear words.

1. 🌟 Product Highlight Post
Describe one product or service offered by the business. Explain how it helps or delights the customer. Use warm, friendly language and end with a soft call-to-action.

2. 🧍Customer Experience or Testimonial Post
Use a real customer review or share a simple story about a customer’s experience. Keep it personal, emotional, or funny if possible. End with an invite or CTA.

3. 🔥 Special Offer or Promo Post
Share details of a current or upcoming promotion. Mention what the customer gets, when it’s available, and why they should act now.

4. 🧑‍🍳 Behind the Scenes Post
Describe what’s going on inside the business: team activities, product-making, preparation, or daily work. Make it feel real and relatable.

5. 🎉 Event or Announcement Post
Announce something special like an event, a launch, or a milestone. Share what it is, when it’s happening, and why customers should be excited.

6. 💬 Helpful Tip or FAQ Post
Answer a common customer question or share a tip related to your product or service. Keep it light and useful.
